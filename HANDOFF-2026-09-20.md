## The finding that produced the demo brick

**Nothing outside the tests ever creates a sky.** `set_sky()` is never called and
`render::Sky{}` is never constructed in any sample, scene or tool. So m17.0's sky has never been
visible in the demo and m17.7b's lighting is inert there — which is also why m17.7b owed a
`docs/perf/` run it could not meaningfully take: the block's cost delta is structurally zero
because the feature is off in it.

**The tuning constraint for m17.7b-demo, which is the point of that brick:** the sky's SH
irradiance REPLACES `blockkit::kAmbient` {0.030, 0.035, 0.055} for every lit surface. Pick sky
colours and `intensity` whose integrated irradiance lands nearby or the whole street changes
exposure and every existing render claim's margin moves. `docs/math/sky-lighting.md` §4 gives the
rule: a uniform sky of radiance L delivers exactly L.

## Two bugs the proof caught that review would not have

1. **`bind_pipeline` silently does nothing for a compute pipeline** — the RHI wants
   `bind_compute_pipeline`. Both dispatches never ran and every consumer quietly kept reading the
   flat ambient constant. The frame looked entirely correct.
2. **The LUT's end-of-frame resource state has no fixed right answer** — ShaderRead when SSR or
   DDGI sampled it, the general layout otherwise. Guessing produced `texture_barrier 'from'
   disagrees with the tracked layout` twice (nine warnings, then two). Fixed with
   `SkyPass::note_skyview_state()`, the owner/reporter split `SdfClipmap::note_level_state`
   already exists for. Now zero.

## One existing assertion changed

`sky_test.cpp`'s m17.0 claim was `differing == 0` — every shaded pixel bit-identical with the sky
on. Honest for a background-only sky, and exactly what m17.7b overturns on purpose. **Not
loosened**: replaced by a sharper claim protecting the same thing — every shaded pixel changes by
the *same factor* ([1.48365, 1.4849] across 741 px of one flat material). A composite bleeding
onto geometry would bleed a gradient and could not hold a constant ratio.

## The demo brick's numbers (m17.7b-demo, `3d535d6`)

The block's sky is **tuned, not chosen**: it delivers ambient (0.0358, 0.0331, 0.0450), luminance
0.0345 against `kAmbient`'s 0.0354 — within 2.5%, which is why all 27 pre-existing claims still
hold unchanged. Intensity is 0.36 because the warm 8-degree sun's forward-scatter lobe dominates
the integral; at 1.0 the same hues give ~2.7x kAmbient.

Claims went 27 → 29. The second new claim was rewritten twice and that is worth knowing:
a luminance A/B (sky on vs off) is nearly worthless here *because* the sky was tuned to match the
constant it displaces — the arms differ by ~3%, mostly tuning error. And the first version measured
a whole-frame mean, where most of the delta was the background being painted rather than the street
being lit. The claim that survived is **colour**: the street's red/blue ratio moves 1.328 → 1.834
under a deliberately red sky, which a flat constant cannot do.

Perf, in `docs/perf/m17.7b-sky/` (regenerate with `pool.py`, never hand-edit):
**render p50 +0.100 ms** (4.300 vs 4.200), the only metric separated from its own noise floor.
render p99 (+0.370) and frame.player p99 (-0.070) are NOT separated and are recorded as
unquotable. 0.10 ms is 0.6% of the ratified 16.600 frame.
