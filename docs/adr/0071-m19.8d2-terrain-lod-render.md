# ADR-0071 (m19.8d2): The terrain LOD chain, drawn — CDLOD selection, exact geomorph, crack-free edges, parent fallback, a pinned root cover

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-06
- Number: provisional (0068 is m19.8c's, on its own branch). Renumber at merge if it clashes.

## Context

ADR-0070 (m19.8d1) cooked a LOD chain: a level-L tile is every second sample of its four
level-(L−1) children, with the same sample count, twice the spacing and one quantisation, and the
manifest carries each tile's descendant `min_y`/`max_y` and a saturated (monotone) geometric
error. ADR-0069 (m19.8a) streams level-0 tiles into fence-safe slots and counts a missing tile as a
hole. This brick draws the chain: which tiles, how they meet, how they switch, and what happens
when the budget cannot hold what the camera wants. Appearance of parents is 8d3's; collision
stays level 0 (ADR-0068). An outside consult (`gpt-6-astra`) proposed the overall scheme (quadtree
of geomipmapped patches, 2:1, stitched edges, morph onto the parent's *triangulated* surface,
pinned coarse cover, pressure lowers detail); each item was checked against the code before use.

## Decision

### 1. CDLOD selection (Strugar 2009) — `rime/render/terrain_lod.hpp`

The quadtree is the manifest's: a level-L tile's children are the four level-(L−1) tiles under it.
Each level gets a morph region `[morph_start_L, morph_end_L]` and a range `range_L`; a level-(L+1)
node splits when the camera is within `range_L` of its 3D box (footprint × `[min_y, max_y]`, which
bounds every vertex of it and of its descendants). The ranges (`terrain_lod_ranges`):

- **Screen error.** A vertical error e at distance d is `e·H / (2·d·tan(fov/2))` pixels; it is
  ≤ τ when d ≥ K·e, `K = H / (2·τ·tan(fov/2))`. With e_L the level's largest cooked error:
  `morph_start_L ≥ K·e_{L+1}` (a vertex only moves toward a surface that is good enough there),
  and an unmorphed level-L leaf only exists beyond `range_{L−1} ≥ K·e_L`.
- **Nesting.** When a level-(L+1) node splits, the camera is within `range_L` of its box, so all of
  it is within `range_L + D_{L+1}` (D = the level's largest box diagonal, height included). Its own
  vertices must not be morphing then, or the split swaps a half-morphed parent for children drawn
  on the unmorphed one. With μ (`step_margin`) the largest per-frame camera step covered:

      morph_start_{L+1} ≥ range_L + D_{L+1} + μ,   morph_end_L = range_L − μ.

  Children of a node that has just split are ≥ `range_L − μ` away, so they appear fully morphed.
- **The formula:** `start_L = max(K·e_{L+1}, range_{L−1} + D_L + μ)`, `end_L = start_L +
  max(D_L, f·start_L)` (f = `morph_fraction`, 0.25), `range_L = end_L + μ`; the top level never
  morphs or splits (+∞).
- **2:1 follows**: a leaf at L sits in a split node y (L+1) within `range_L`; a neighbour leaf at
  ≥ L+2 is unsplit, so wholly beyond `range_{L+1}`; but it touches y, so part of it is within
  `range_L + D_{L+1} < range_{L+1}`. (Proof (c) measures it: zero balance collapses on every ideal
  frame.)

Selection is a pure function of (camera, ranges, `usable`). A node draws its four children only
if all four are usable; otherwise it draws itself (a **fallback**). Fallback can break 2:1, so a
**balance pass** collapses every split node with a same-level edge neighbour that is not in the
tree; collapses only coarsen, every node on a path from a root is drawable, and the result is the
unique largest balanced subtree (balanced subtrees are closed under union), so it is order-free.
Each leaf carries four **coarser-edge bits**.

### 2. The geomorph — exact, onto the parent's triangulation (`terrain.vert`)

With ADR-0060's diagonal (i, j)→(i+1, j+1) at every level, a child's odd vertices lie on parent
triangle edges: (odd, even) and (even, odd) on cell edges, (odd, odd) on the parent diagonal
between (i−1, j−1) and (i+1, j+1). Each is its segment's midpoint in x and z, so its parent height
is the **average of its two even neighbours** — no parent fetch, and only y moves. At morph 1 every
child triangle has its three vertices in one parent triangle (checked cell by cell for the four
child cells of a parent cell), so the child mesh *is* the parent surface. Morph is
`clamp((|v − camera| − start) / (end − start), 0, 1)` per **vertex**, from its unmorphed world
position. The LOD path places vertices by **global sample index**, `origin + float(base + i)·cell_L`
(power-of-two cells make level L and L+1 agree too), with `round` on the unorm fetch and `precise`
arithmetic, so a vertex two tiles share is bit-identical and the morph-0 heights equal the CPU's
f32 bits. `(1 − m)·own + m·target` makes both endpoints exact. Normals: the fragment stage's
derivative normal of the morphed position — the drawn triangle's plane, the parent's at morph 1,
continuous wherever positions are.

### 3. Crack-free edges

`TerrainPush` gains `lod_origin`, `lod_camera`, `lod_tile` (base index, flags, level): **208 B**
(the max_push_constant_bytes refusal path is kept; both test GPUs report 256). Bit k set = the
neighbour across that edge is coarser; those vertices take m = 1, putting each odd one on the coarse
edge. Residual: the average rounds once (½ ULP of y) and the vertex's along-edge position may sit
off the midpoint by its own rounding (½ ULP of s, times the slope); measured 9.5e-7 m.

### 4. Residency: pressure lowers detail, never coverage (`TerrainResidency`, chain worlds only)

- **Pinned root cover**: reserved slots (a non-root takes a free slot only while more are free than
  roots waiting), never evicted for a load; a root cover over budget is refused at construction,
  counted (`root_cover_refusals`), and draws nothing.
- **Wanted** = the ideal selection's leaves + ancestors + roots; **prefetch** = children of an ideal
  leaf within one level-diagonal of the range that would split it.
- **Priority**, for loads and eviction: roots, wanted, prefetch; then by the distance of the tile's
  **parent** box; coarser first. *(A choice beyond the spec, measured:* ranking by a tile's own
  distance let near fine tiles starve the siblings a split needs — 10 slots drew only one level;
  ranked by parent box they draw three.) A tile may evict any resident non-root of lower priority.
- **Drawn** = the selection over resident tiles. `fallback_draws` counts tile-frames drawn coarser
  than selected, where 8a counted holes; parents use the flat material (`fallback_appearance_draws`).
- **8d1 handoff**: a child waits for its resident parent (which keeps its CPU heightfield while
  resident) and is compared with `samples_coincide`; a mismatch refuses the parent. *(Beyond the
  spec:)* a level-0 child's verdict is final; a coarser child's is **provisional** and retracted if
  that child is refused by its own children — measured: a corrupted level-1 tile first blamed the
  innocent root.

A level-0 world keeps m19.8a's path exactly.

## Consequences and known limits

- A coarse tile is drawn where a fine one is missing; coverage holes only from refusals.
- **A provisional refusal of a root** leaves its area uncovered until it is reloaded (2 frames
  measured), counted in `uncovered_draws`.
- Parents keep their CPU heightfield while resident (pair checks): CPU memory ≈ GPU memory for
  parents. Unmeasured at real sizes.
- No frustum culling; one raster pass per leaf; selection uses `std::set` and is O(nodes · sweeps).
- The error is vertical (ADR-0070); the ranges use each level's **largest** error and diagonal.
- LOD positions come from the grid, not the tile's own origin (equal for a cooked world;
  `check_tile` allows 1 mm).

## What 8d3 / 8e must do

- **8d3**: bake parent appearance and blend it through the same morph (endpoint-identical shading);
  replace the flat placeholder (`fallback_appearance_draws` should reach 0).
- **8e**: measure CPU/GPU ms, upload bandwidth and bytes per slot; frustum culling; batch leaves into
  one pass; tune τ, μ, morph fraction and prefetch for travel speeds; a byte budget.
