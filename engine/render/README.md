# engine/render — the render graph & high-level renderer (M5)

`rime::render` is "where the picture comes from" ([ARCHITECTURE.md](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)):
the layer that turns a *described frame* into recorded RHI commands. Its foundation is the
**render graph** ([ADR-0019](../../docs/adr/0019-render-graph.md)) — each frame, render code
declares passes and the resources they read/write; the graph compiles the execution order,
culls dead work, allocates transients from a cross-frame cache, emits every between-pass barrier
through the RHI's `texture_barrier` seam, and records the passes bracketed by GPU timestamps and
debug labels. This is the structure that makes UE5-class techniques tractable, and it is "the
home for M10": GI, virtual shadow maps, and many-lights arrive later as passes and resources on
this graph, not as a renderer rewrite.

| m10.5a | **DDGI probes — the trace-and-store half** — a camera-centred, texel-snapped lattice of irradiance probes, each sphere-traced through the SDF clipmap every update (spherical-Fibonacci rays, a fresh random rotation per frame, SDF-based self-shadowing, a grey-world albedo — real per-surface colour is a named follow-up), folded into a temporally-blended octahedral irradiance atlas + a Chebyshev-visibility atlas (border-correct — `docs/math/ddgi.md` §6) via hysteresis. | m10.5b | **DDGI probes — consume + react, and the M10 thesis proof** — `pbr_forward_shadowed.frag` samples both atlases as an indirect-diffuse term: an 8-probe trilinear cage (CLAMP_TO_EDGE, not extrapolated, past the lattice's own edge), weighted by the Chebyshev visibility test (stops a lit probe leaking through solid geometry onto a fragment on the far side) and the standard wrap/backface term. `DdgiProbes::invalidate` is the destruction-reactive hysteresis override (0.5 for 5 updates, vs. the ~30-frame default) — the last of the six ADR-0032 coupling contracts this milestone needed. `tests/render/gi_thesis_test.cpp` is the executable version of the milestone's own headline sentence: a floor patch lit only by DDGI reads dim-but-clearly-nonzero, then materially brightens (and its *isolated indirect contribution* measurably moves, not just the direct term) once the occluding wall is removed and a handful of fast-tracked updates run. `docs/math/ddgi.md` §10–§13 has the full derivation. | landed |
| m10.6 | **GI integration — the traced field replaces the ambient hack** — with DDGI on, `pbr_forward_shadowed.frag` now *substitutes* the traced indirect term for M5.6's flat ambient constant instead of adding to it: the sky the constant approximated already enters the probes through their escaped rays, so summing both double-counts it (the "ambient + GI" wash-out). Off, the shader is byte-for-byte the M5.6 baseline (the regression bridge, ADR-0032 §11). Adds the two-room walls-fall proof (`gi_thesis_test.cpp`): a covered dark room lit *only* by bounce from a sunlit room next door lights up ≈8.6× when the divider falls, while the constant-ambient control stays bit-identical — GI as the whole signal, not a correction. Derivation in `docs/math/pbr.md` §6.1. Colour bleed (grey-world albedo), indirect specular (m10.7), and a probe/indirect debug view (view-mode plumbing, m10.8) remain named follow-ups. | landed |
| m10.7a | **the thin SSR G-buffer** — with `ssr_enabled`, the shadowed forward pass writes a second colour attachment (octahedral world normal in RG, perceptual roughness in B, a geometry mask in A) that m10.7b's SSR march consumes. One shader source, two SPIR-V modules: a new `rime_add_shader_variant` compiles `pbr_forward_shadowed.frag` a second time with `-DWRITE_GBUFFER`, so the baseline module carries no unused second output (validation-clean) — the MRT the RHI has supported since M5.1, first used here. Off (default): the G-buffer is never allocated or written, byte-identical baseline (ADR-0032 §11). `gbuffer_test.cpp` decodes a rendered pixel's normal (a flat quad and a 25°-tilted one, dot > 0.997 vs ground truth) and its per-surface roughness (0.85 vs 0.30). | landed |
| m10.7b | **the SSR resolve** — a fullscreen pass (`ssr_resolve.frag`) that reconstructs each surface from the depth buffer + the m10.7a G-buffer, reflects the view ray about the normal, and fixed-step **linear-marches** it through the depth buffer in view space; on a shallow crossing (within a tuned `ssr_thickness`) it samples the frame's own colour, else falls back to the flat ambient the forward pass uses. Fresnel-weighted, roughness-faded (a sharp mirror sample is wrong for a rough surface, so SSR fades out — cone-blur resolve is m10.7c), edge-faded against the screen border. Writes a second HDR target the tonemap reads, so SSR-off is byte-identical (ADR-0032 §11). `docs/math/ssr.md` derives the march, the thickness problem, the miss classification, and the artifacts v1 accepts (screen-edge cutoff, thickness error, no rough/temporal — all named, not chased). `ssr_test.cpp`: a smooth dark floor's mirror pixel brightens ≈28× onto the cube's known reflection point, a matte floor's does not, and a sky-facing floor pixel does not (reflection, not global lift). | landed |
| m10.7c | **the probe fallback + roughness cone** — the two follow-ups m10.7b named. A ray that leaves the screen, hits the background, or belongs to a rough surface now samples the **DDGI probe field** (m10.5) in the *reflection direction* instead of a flat sky constant — reflections everywhere, coupled to the GI thesis (a reflective floor in the room a broken wall just lit picks that light up the same frame). The roughness fade becomes a **cone**: the reflection blends from the sharp screen hit toward that (pre-integrated, over-blurred) field as roughness rises, so a rough surface reflects a *blurred world* rather than a black hole. Sampling irradiance as directional specular is an over-blurring approximation (`docs/math/ssr.md` §5.1 says what it gets wrong); a true pre-filtered specular probe, a hi-Z acceleration march (m12.0 — perf only, unmeasurable on lavapipe), and the mid-roughness screen mip-blur (needs an RHI mip/LOD surface) are named follow-ups. DDGI off ⇒ the fallback is the flat ambient again ⇒ byte-for-byte m10.7b (ADR-0032 §11). `ssr_test.cpp`: a reflective floor reflects a sunlit wall present *only as an SDF twin* (invisible to the screen, so the reflection can only be the fallback) — smooth (2.3×) and rough (1.9×) floors both brighten with DDGI on, where m10.7b's rough floor showed nothing. | landed |
| m13.1a | **the FX particle draw pass** — Track FX brick fx1a's load-bearing half (ADR-0035 §5). Additive HDR billboards for the deterministic CPU sim `engine/vfx` has run since M8.4 and nothing ever drew. Six vertices synthesised per particle from `gl_VertexIndex`, the particle array read straight out of a storage buffer by `gl_InstanceIndex` — no vertex buffer, no index buffer, no per-particle mesh. Depth-tested, depth-write **off**, additive (so no sorting is needed: additive blending is commutative, which is the one blend mode where the transparency-ordering problem does not arise). Off by default and off is **structural** — the pass is never declared, so there is no bind, no barrier, no attachment load, and the frame is byte-identical to a build without the file (ADR-0032 §11's discipline, applied to FX). `fx_particle_test.cpp` lands the **coverage-delta pixel proof M8.4 deferred**: on-screen radiance and `DustField::coverage()` rise together on a burst and decay together as the puff ages (measured 20.27 → 3.64 against 0.078 → 0.011), the gate-off frame is byte-identical, `intensity = 0` is provably *not* the same state as the gate being off, and the draw budget truncates loudly. The three effect families and the destruction/weapon event glue land beside it as **m13.1b** (in [`rime::vfx`](../vfx/README.md) and the consumer respectively). | landed |
| m13.2a | **view-frustum culling, with the ledger counters ADR-0035 §2a asks for.** Extraction emitted *literally every* `{WorldTransform, MeshRef, MaterialRef}` — fine at six walls, not at a block of per-part render leaves, most of them behind the camera at any moment. Gribb–Hartmann planes from the frame's own `view_proj` (near is `row2` alone, because this is **Vulkan** clip space where z runs 0..w), a conservative world AABB per draw from per-mesh local bounds computed at upload, and the positive-vertex test. **It PARTITIONS rather than deletes:** one draw list feeds the camera passes *and* both shadow passes, so deleting culled entries stops off-screen occluders casting into the view — measured, when the first version did exactly that and `gi_thesis_test`'s covered room went from 0.04 to 0.74 while every other proof stayed green. Camera passes take the visible prefix; shadow passes take the whole list; `record_draws` indexes by loop position, so every parallel array stays aligned by construction. The proof is **"on renders byte-identical pixels to off"** rather than the M10 gate discipline's "off is the baseline", because culling is not a technique that changes the picture — plus `culled >= 40 of 65`, so a cull that silently stopped culling could not pass. | landed |
| m19.3 | **the terrain heightfield draws** (ADR-0062) — `TerrainPass` uploads a cooked `HeightfieldAsset`'s u16 samples verbatim as the new `rhi::Format::R16Unorm` and draws the grid with a VERTEX PULLER: no vertex buffer at all, `gl_VertexIndex` is a grid coordinate, `texelFetch` reads the sample and the stage re-evaluates ADR-0060 §1's `offset + scale * q` — so no second height representation exists anywhere in the engine, which is what makes the drawn surface and the collided surface the same surface. The index buffer carries ADR-0060's cell diagonal (a non-planar cell has a different surface under the other one) and `terrain.frag`'s normal is the drawn triangle's own plane normal from `dFdx/dFdy`, faceted exactly as the contacts physics reports are. Owned like the FX pass and removable without breaking the build; an unknown tile declares no pass at all. `tests/terrain_render/terrain_height_test.cpp` compares the GPU-reconstructed height against a `rime::physics` raycast at 36 positions — worst 9.5 µm against a 1 mm margin derived from the R16_UNORM round trip, with a flipped cell diagonal rejected at 36/36 probes by up to 97 mm. No LOD, no splat blending, no streaming (all deferred in the ADR). | landed |
| m19.5 | **terrain shades with the GGX BRDF** (ADR-0064) — `terrain.frag` includes the new shared `brdf.glsl` (also used by both forward shaders) instead of Lambert, and `TerrainLayer`/`TerrainLight` carry `metallic` and `roughness`, blended across the four splat layers in the same difference form as base colour so the equal-layers bit-identity anchor holds. The push block is 160 bytes, checked against the new `rhi::AdapterInfo::max_push_constant_bytes` (a device below it refuses every upload, counted and warned once); `terrain_push` and `TerrainPass::add` now take the camera `eye`. Ambient is a uniform-environment stand-in, so a metal has no sky reflections yet. | landed |
| m19.6 | **terrain reflects the sky** (ADR-0065) — `TerrainPass::add` takes an optional `SkyLightBinding` (the `add_shadowed` convention; absent = the pass's own dummy LUT and zero SH, so terrain matches a frozen copy of m19.5's shader to one f16 ULP per channel — two separately compiled programs, so not bit-identical on every driver: RADV differs by one ULP at 2 pixels). With a live sky, `sky_sh_irradiance(n)` replaces the flat ambient for the diffuse and the sky-view LUT along `reflect(-v, n)` (clamped to the horizon) is the specular environment, faded toward the SH by alpha = roughness² and weighted by Karis' analytic EnvBRDFApprox — no new LUT. The caller reports the LUT's `ShaderRead` state back to `SkyPass`. No prefiltered radiance and no sky occlusion (valleys reflect sky they cannot see). | landed |
| m19.6b | **metals take no diffuse ambient on the forward PBR pass** (ADR-0065 addendum) — `pbr_forward_shadowed.frag` weights the flat constant, the sky SH and the DDGI branch by `(1 − metallic)`, and mirrors the sky-view LUT (binding 18) through `env_brdf_approx` (moved to `brdf.glsl`) **only in the non-G-buffer variant**: with SSR on, `ssr_resolve` already adds the specular environment, so the `-DWRITE_GBUFFER` twin compiles the term out. Limits: SSR reflects metals at the dielectric Fresnel; DDGI-on/SSR-off metals get no ambient; the baseline `pbr_forward.frag` is untouched. | landed |
| m19.7b | **terrain samples per-layer albedo+height textures** (ADR-0066 addendum) — `TerrainLayer` gains a borrowed `albedo_height` texture handle (the builder resolves it through `GpuAssetBridge`, as every material texture is) and a per-layer `uv_scale` in metres per repeat. Four separate 2-D bindings (5–8, trilinear, `REPEAT`), not a texture array, because layers are independently cooked assets of different sizes. Each layer's colour is `base_color * texture(world.xz / uv_scale).rgb`, then m19.4's difference-form blend, unchanged. A layer without a texture binds a pass-owned 1×1 white `RGBA8_SRGB` texel, so every earlier anchor stays bit-exact. **World** XZ, not tile-local, so a pattern runs straight across tile seams. The splat UBO grows to 144 bytes, appended, so the frozen m19.5 reference shader still reads it. A non-finite or ≤ 0 `uv_scale` is refused and counted. The heights (A, measured linear through the sRGB format) are sampled here and first used by m19.7c. No anisotropy, and no engine-side terrain builder yet. | landed |
| m19.7c | **height-blended splat transitions** (ADR-0066 m19.7c addendum; fix 1 in its addendum) — `TerrainLayer` gains `height_contrast` (finite, ≥ 0, else refused and counted), carried as one more appended `vec4` in the splat UBO (160 bytes). `terrain.frag`'s `height_blend()` redistributes the painted weights by the heights the layer textures carry: effective height `e_k = c_k·h_k`, `g_k = exp2(e_k − e_ref)` with `e_ref` the highest e over **painted** (`w > 0`) layers, `b = w·g / Σ w·g`, then m19.4's difference-form blend on `b`. A bypass returns the sampled weights themselves when every painted layer has one effective height or none has contrast, so untextured terrain and contrast 0 are bit-identical to m19.7b, and equal contrasts at equal heights are too. A layer painted at weight 0 never gains a share, and a layer's share is strictly monotone in its painted weight (both proven). A tall layer painted at 1/255 moves the others by at most its own share (proven; the green change at contrast 2/6/6 fell from 0.127 to 0.030, bound 0.066). Unequal contrasts are biased by design: at equal heights the higher-contrast layer takes more of the pixel (proven against a CPU model, within 1 f16 ULP). Measured earlier: a 0.498 texel becomes 0.798 where the layer is high and 0.198 where it is low, at contrast 4. No triplanar mapping, no per-layer normal maps, 8-bit height, and still no engine-side terrain builder. | landed |
| m19.8a | **terrain streams on the GPU** (ADR-0069) — `TerrainResidency` draws a `TerrainWorld` (the new `rime::assets` grid manifest) from a constructor-fixed budget of tile slots chosen around the camera: activation/retention radii with hysteresis, heightfields requested and released through the AssetServer's streamed handles, synchronous uploads nearest-first, farthest-beyond-retention eviction on demand. Eviction is fence-safe by m18.5's rules — `TerrainSlotTable` bumps the generation at evict and frees the slot only when the in-order retirement watermark reaches its last reader — and a stale `TerrainResidentId` declares no pass (memcmp-proven). `TerrainLayerBuilder` resolves palettes by manifest kind (Material → scalars; TerrainLayer → scalars + texture + uv_scale + contrast), uploading each shared layer texture once and destroying it with its last palette. `TerrainPass` gains `release`/`tile_bytes`. Missing tiles are holes, counted per reason (not_loaded / no_free_slot / upload_failed / refused); no coarse cover or LOD until m19.8d. Plateau measured: 333 frames over 16 tiles, 4 slots, tile bytes capped at 4 × 7448. | landed |
| m19.8d3 | **coarse terrain has an appearance** (ADR-0072) — `TerrainPass::set_bake` gives a parent tile its cooked bake (base colour sRGB + metallic/roughness, one texel per sample); `terrain.frag` reads it by `texelFetch` with a hand-written bilinear in sample space (exact at a vertex) and fades every tile toward its PARENT's bake by the per-vertex geomorph factor `terrain.vert` now outputs, so at morph 1 a tile shades exactly as its parent and a level switch changes no pixel (measured 0; 0.92 without). `TerrainResidency` loads bakes with the heightfield, binds the parent's for each child draw (a reader of both slots), and counts every shortfall instead of refusing to draw (`fallback_appearance_draws` is 0 for a fully cooked world). `TerrainLodLeaf::coarser_corners` holds a corner touching a coarser tile at morph 1. Push block unchanged at 208 B. | landed |
| m19.8d2 | **terrain draws its LOD chain** (ADR-0071) — `terrain_lod.hpp` selects CDLOD leaves from the 8d1 manifest (per-level ranges from geometric error, viewport and FOV; nesting inequality; a counted 2:1 balance pass; coarser-edge bits per leaf). `terrain.vert` gains a LOD path: vertices placed by global sample index (bit-identical where tiles share them), odd vertices geomorphed per vertex onto the parent triangulation (average of two even neighbours), coarser edges forced to morph 1; the m19.3 path is untouched. `TerrainPush` grows to 208 B (both test GPUs report 256). `TerrainResidency` drives a world with a chain by selection: (level, x, z) records, pinned root cover with reserved slots, wanted = ideal leaves + ancestors, prefetch one level finer, slots ranked by parent box, `samples_coincide` per pair on arrival (a mismatch refuses the parent), fallback draws counted where m19.8a counted holes, parents drawn with the flat placeholder material (8d3 bakes their appearance). A level-0 world runs m19.8a's path unchanged. | landed |
| m19.8e | **terrain streams within budgets** (ADR-0073) — `TerrainResidencyConfig` gains a **byte budget** (tiles occupied *and retiring*, by `TerrainPass::tile_bytes`, plus the builder's layer textures; the pinned root cover reserved inside it; a world whose roots exceed it refused and counted), a **per-frame upload cap** (the first tile that would cross it closes it for the frame, so nothing farther overtakes it; waits counted and optionally journalled), and opt-in **frustum culling** of the selection's leaves by their cooked boxes (a culled leaf's slot is still marked read, so residency is identical with culling on or off). Admission is by `TerrainPass::predicted_tile_bytes`, computed before allocating; `TerrainLayerBuilder::update` takes an allowance so a palette never creates a layer texture past either limit. Appearance bakes now load on the AssetServer's streamed path and are released once uploaded. Measured by `samples/15-terrain`. | landed |

Deliberate v0 bounds (ADR-0019 records why): serial recording on one queue (pass boundaries keep
the parallel-recording and async-compute seams open) and no transient aliasing (measure first).
Buffers joined the graph as first-class resources at m10.3, with clustered forward's light lists as
the first GPU-driven consumer ADR-0019 predicted.

## Layout

```
include/rime/render/   # public interface
  render_graph.hpp     #   RenderGraph, RGTexture, RGBuffer, pass descriptors
  lighting/            #   the M10 lighting techniques (shadows, clustered, …)
src/                   # the compiler + executor
```

Depends on `rime::rhi` only (the scene layer adds `rime::ecs` at M5.5). Tests live in
`tests/render/` — pixel-verified, GPU-free on lavapipe like every proof since M3.3.
